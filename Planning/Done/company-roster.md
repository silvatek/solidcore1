# Company roster

The company lists Sam, Alex, Max, Morgan, and Quinn. The catalog meshes are Viking (the captain), Assassin, and Blacksmith. Sam, Morgan, and Quinn use Assassin. Alex and Max use Blacksmith. On spawn, a companion replaces that catalog mesh with a low-poly body on the Viking skeleton. The captain stays on the Viking sculpt. Each companion stores a matte shirt color, a matte trouser color, and a light pink skin color for the head and hands. Sam's shirt is red and his trousers are blue. Alex's shirt is blue and his trousers are red. A face is drawn on the front of the head. Each companion also has a vocation: Warrior, Rogue, Mage, or Cleric. Sam is a Rogue and Morgan is a Mage. The nameplate adds that initial in brackets, such as Sam (R). The party remembers who is selected from that list (Sam and Alex to start). Only as many as the current party limit walk with the captain. Dragongate raises that limit to five. Build party can add the rest; the selection still starts as Sam and Alex.
