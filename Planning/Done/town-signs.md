# Town signs

A named town gets one welcome sign at the middle of its cells: one thin dark-brown pole, a flat light-brown board, white letters with a black border. "Welcome to" is the first line and the town name is the second. The board faces south, on that middle, unless that town sets another spot. A single cell's middle is the cell center. Dragongate's sign stands on the north edge of the town, east of the gate, and faces north, so it can be read while facing south. The gate stays clear.
