
- Lairs will generally have an entrance and an exit
- Exits will be locked from the outside
- Most lairs will be procedurally generated and so have a different layout for every playthrough
- Lairs may have multiple levels
- Lairs should consist of rooms and corridors
- Each lair level should have a size (x and y dimensions) and complexity (number of dead-ends)
- Lairs will contain monsters, getting tougher as you progress through levels
