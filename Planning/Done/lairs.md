# Lairs

Closed visits, apart from the open world, for fights that are still to come. The first is the **Iglin tunnels**, a stone maze from the mouth south of Iglin (`X`) to the mouth east of Relion (`Y`), so the broken bridge can be left alone. For early testing the two mouths are also joined by a straight run, and the side passages stay. Each mouth is the cave's stone frame on its own cliff, facing the grass beside that cell. A click on the dark opening enters, and a click on an opening inside leaves by that end. The captain goes alone. Companions stand on the grass in front of that mouth when he leaves. The mouths stay shut until Driven from the village has fired. Until then a click does nothing. F10 Enter cave does not make this trip. The portal list can stand the squad on the grass just north of the X mouth, and that records Driven from the village. The tunnels are dark. A dim light sits at each mouth, and the middle has none. A torch in either hand burns at the captain and lights the stone around him. A torch left in the pack does not. His body does not cast a shadow in the tunnels, so the flame is not sealed inside him. Design notes for later lairs are in `Content/Lairs.md`.
