# Main menu

F10 opens it. **1 Use portal** lists the towns and then the Iglin tunnels. A town moves the party onto that town and fires its enter-town event. Iglin tunnels stands the party on the grass just north of the X mouth and records Driven from the village. **2 Test Drill** runs the formation drill. **3 Journal** opens the journal. **4 Credits** opens the credits page (Silvatek, Cursor + Grok, Fab Viking, grass, Assassin, and Blacksmith). **5 Build party** lists the company. It stays dim until events allow at least two companions. Each row toggles that companion in or out, up to the current party limit. **6 Enter cave** stays dim until Reach broken bridge opens the cave. It then loads the Meagre Dungeon interior for the captain alone, and reads **Leave cave** inside. **7 Character editor** shows one companion on a plain full-screen background. Page Up and Page Down cycle Sam, Alex, Max, Morgan, and Quinn. Up and Down select height, build, or gender. Left and Right change the selected value, and the pointer can drag that slider. A panel in the corner lists that companion's proportions and shirt, trouser, and skin colors as numbers. F10 or Esc returns to the open world. **8 Inventory** opens the inventory. Up/Down, Enter, and the pointer also work on the menu: resting the pointer on a row highlights it, and a left click chooses it. The same pointer works on the portal list and the party builder. Esc or F10 closes. Move, look, and battle-plan keys are ignored while the menu, the portal list, the party builder, the journal, the inventory, the credits page, or the character editor is open.
