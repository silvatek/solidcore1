# Basic combat

Status: New

## Story

This story depends on [Early game battle plans](early-game-battle-plans.md), which is Ready, and [Basic monsters](basic-monsters.md), which is Ready. Those stories ship first.

The player fights the three reptiles on the grass beside the Iglin bridge. During the fight the player moves the captain and switches among the battle plans the squad has. The squad's members, their equipment, and the battle plan in use decide the outcome. [Basic monsters](basic-monsters.md) leaves a click on those reptiles with the fight unstarted. This story is that fight.
