# Bridges

Wherever a route crosses a river, a bridge stands on that water: an up ramp, a flat center, and a down ramp. The center is as wide as that road and as long as the water, with a short rest on each bank, and it is its own mesh. Each bridge has a broken flag, false unless that route joins Iglin and Relion. That crossing starts with the center undrawn, and each ramp ends in a solid face. The gap in front of that face cannot be walked. Entering Relion repairs it: the center is drawn, and the crossing can be used. Its ramps still meet each bank. Every other crossing keeps its center. Each ramp runs down to its own bank and continues into the ground, so a high bank and a low bank get different lengths. The walking surface uses the road brown, and it is the triangle surface, so a character walks up a ramp, across an intact center, and down the other side. The bridge follows the painted strip, which lies half a terrain step southwest of the cell centres. The water underneath keeps the river texture. Characters stop at the sea, at a mountain, and at the river channel. The channel is the 4 m width, so the rest of a river cell can still be walked. An intact bridge is the only way across that channel.
