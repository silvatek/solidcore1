
# Geography

The explorable world is bounded between seas on the east & west, with impassable mountains to the north and south. The New Kingdom sits in a valley in the northern mountains, and the great Dragons hold power in the southern mountains.

## Towns and Cities

### Jethan

Ancient capital city of the old kingdom, now a desolate and dangerous ruin.

### Kanfold

The current capital of the new kingdom, high up in the northern valley. Generally peaceful and properous, but something lurks in the catacombes and caves below.

### Relion

A town in the new kingdom. Smaller than Kanfold, but larger than Iglin. Used to be the capital of the New Kingdom before it moved to Kanfold.

### Iglin

A small village in a valley spur. Reliant on a single bridge across a wild mountain river for access to the rest of the world.

Iglin is where the orphan (who will later go on to become the Captain) was raised.

### Visolar

An island city off the north eastern coast; survived the fall of Jethan, but has remained largely  isolated from the New Kingdom.


# Characters

## People

### The Captain

Also known as the Orphan, Outcast, Vagrant or Ranger, depending on progression. The central character in the story, and the leader of a company of adventurers.

### Jarth (NPC)

A master blacksmith. Rumoured to know how to forge the metal from the solid core of Dracosan's egg.

### Melican (NPC)

Queen of the New Kingdom. Rules from her palace in Kanfold.

### Rethil (NPC)

Leads the villager's initial attempt to retake the bridge, killed in battle along with the Orphan. Unlike the Orphan, Rethil remains dead.

### Urlar (historical figure)

The master sorcerer who turned the core of Dracosan's egg to solid metal, but died in the attempt.

## Dragons

### Dracothar

The first known dragon. Destroyed the old kingdom, then killed by a great sorcerer.

### Dracosan

Second dragon lord. Attacked the new kingdom but defeated.

### Dracorex

Third dragon lord. Still growing its power in the southern mountains as-of 200 AtF.


# World Time

The Journal records entries using "world time". 

World time starts on day 45 of year 200 AtF ("200/45 AtF"). The in-game date is incremented every time a new play session is started. Every 100 game days the year increments and the day resets to 0.

The in-game time is the hours and minutes since the start of the current play session, where in-game hours and minutes are identical to real-world ones.

An example journal entry timestamp is "01:24, 200/53 AtF". This is 84 minutes into the 9th play session.

# History

Note: dates are before (BtF) / after (AtF) the fall of Jethan. The orphan was born some time between 140 and 180 AtF.

- __200 BtF__

    The old kingdom is based in a great city (Jethan) in the middle of fertile plains, mountains to the north west, ocean to north east.

- __40 BtF__

    The first wyvern appears in the far south west, and attacks the kingdom. 

- __38 BtF__

    Wyvern defeated.

- __30 BtF__

    First dragon (Dracothar) born, the offspring of a basilisk and a wyvern

- __25 BtF__

    Dracothar learn to corrupt insects

- __20 BtF__

     Dracothar attacks with an army of draconids and insectoids, and starts pushing into the kingdom. The kingdom ignores the problem until it is too late, and monsters start flooding in. There is a long war, with the kingdom slowly losing ground.

- __5 BtF__

    Guilds are formed to investigate the Dragon’s magical powers.

- __0 AtF__

    Jethan eventually falls and is overrun and ruined. The old kingdom’s surviving people keep falling back to the north, and are eventually pushed up into the mountains. 

- __3 AtF__

    They find a valley with a narrow entrance that they can fortify, and manage to keep the monsters out. The valley becomes the new kingdom, with the main town (Relion) half way up where the valley splits. Smaller towns & villages established even further up the valleys.

- __15 AtF__

    Big battle at the entrance to the valley, and Dracothar is killed. Most of the old kingdom remains overrun by monsters, but the valley remains safe.

- __18 AtF__

    Dragons lose control of some of the corrupted insects

- __140 AtF__

    A new dragon, Dracosan, rises to command of the monsters. It attacks the fort and breaks into the valley, pushing up to the Relion before being defeated.  Due to this close call, the capital is moved to Kanfold right up at the top of the valley. The fort at the bottom is strengthened and relative peace returns.

    Dracosan guards an egg prophesised to have an even more powerful dragon. 

    A master sorcerer, Urlar, manages to turn the core of the egg into solid metal to stop it hatching. The master sorcerer, and hundreds of other mages and warriors, are killed to make this happen.

- __170 AtF__

    The new kingdom starts to send troops out of the valley to reclaim the wilderness. 

- __190 AtF__

    New walled towns are established to maintain a foothold in parts of the wilderness, but without the fortifications of the valley kingdom, monsters still roam.

    There are constant skirmishes between troops and monsters.

    Further south the monsters dominate. 

- __195 AtF__

    A new dragon lord, Dracorex, starts to establish its power in the Southern 
    Mountains.

- __200 AtF__

    Monsters destroy the bridge in the spur valley, cutting off the village of Iglin.
