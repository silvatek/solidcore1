# Iglin Bridge Fight Enhancements

Status: Ready

## Story

The captain starts in Iglin. Three Small raptors wait on the grass beside the broken bridge. The player can leave them alone. This is an open world. When the captain comes into the fight, those three kill the captain. The captain may kill one of them first. That death still comes, and running away does not end the fight.

Any death of the captain, at any point in the game, lands them in their current stronghold without the gear they had when they died. This fight is the first time that happens. Until that death, the company stronghold stays locked, the Iglin tunnels stay locked, and the broken bridge still blocks the river. The current stronghold, once that death opens it, is the cave. A later story adds the NPC Rethil, who guides the player toward the bridge. This story does not.

Reach broken bridge is removed. Standing on the bridge records nothing and shows nothing.

The first time the captain dies, FirstDeath fires. Like every event, it fires once. A later death does not fire it again. FirstDeath disables Starter and enables Attack and Defend. Driven from the village requires FirstDeath.

This story depends on [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md), which is Done. That story ships first. [Combat preparation](Done/combat-preparation.md) is Done, and it ships before Basic combat.

This story introduces no game concept. Raptor, the encounter, the captain's death, and the event list are already named. FirstDeath is another event on that list.

## Acceptance

- The Iglin Bridge encounter still places three Small raptors on the Iglin side of the broken Iglin–Relion bridge, as [Basic monsters](Done/basic-monsters.md) placed them.
- The fight starts as [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) says, when the captain comes into DetectionRange. Staying outside that range leaves the raptors idle. From the moment the fight starts, the three Small raptors kill the captain. Killing one of them leaves the fight as those three, and the fight ends on the captain's death.
- [Leave a fight](leave-a-fight.md) is where EscapeDistance ends a fight. This encounter does not end. The three Small raptors keep fighting until the captain dies.
- Any death of the captain, in this fight or later, anywhere in the game, uses one landing. The captain wakes in the company stronghold they have then, without the gear they had when they died. The first time, that stronghold is the cave. The wake is the one [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) specifies: Rags on the body, the rest of the inventory emptied, and the companions who were out despawned. The leave uses that story's Build squad check. The gear on the ground is [Gear left at the death](gear-left-at-the-death.md).
- This bridge death is that landing's first use. A later captain death uses the same landing, in whatever stronghold the company has by then.
- Until that death, the cave mouth stays shut, the Iglin tunnels stay shut, and the Iglin–Relion bridge stays broken. The river still cannot be crossed there, so the wider world stays out of reach. The death opens the stronghold. The tunnels still wait on Driven from the village. The bridge still waits on Enter Relion.
- This story does not add Rethil. A later story adds that NPC, who guides the player toward the bridge.
- Reach broken bridge is removed. Standing on either ramp of the Iglin bridge records nothing and shows nothing. Reaching the bridge does not show "You are killed by monsters".
- FirstDeath fires when the captain dies. It is ACT_0. The journal names it `First death`. It fires once. A second death leaves it unfired and leaves its changes as they are.
- FirstDeath disables Starter and enables Attack and Defend. The squad's selection becomes Attack, then Defend. The active plan becomes Attack. The battle-plan list shows F1 Attack and F2 Defend. The journal records `Starter disabled`, `Attack enabled`, and `Defend enabled`.
- Driven from the village requires FirstDeath. It does not require Killed by monsters. The portal to the grass north of the Iglin Tunnels Entrance still records Driven from the village when FirstDeath has not fired.
- Killed by monsters no longer disables Starter and no longer enables Attack or Defend. The gear they lose, and the wake in the current stronghold, stay on every captain death. FirstDeath does not take that landing. [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) still enables Meagre on a death.
- Enter Relion still repairs the bridge. The center is drawn. The road stays `Iglin -> Relion, track`.
- The product commit bumps `SOLID_BUILD_ID` and `SOLID_BUILD_NOTE`.

## Implementation notes

- The catalog row stays three Small raptors. It is the `FStaticEncounter` named Iglin Bridge in `SolidEncounter::StaticEncounters`: one group, size Small, count 3, plan Attack, and no event id. The fight adds no member. [Place encounter markers](Done/place-encounter-markers.md) can still move the focus onto a chart mark. The band, the separation, and the count of three stay.
- `SolidCore1.Battle.IglinBridge` already runs this fight. The squad plan is Starter, the loadout is `SolidInventory::StartingLoadout`, and the group is that row. The second spear kills one Small. The next claw kills the captain. The other two abort. The group's hide stays above 0. `AdvanceBattle` calls `WakeFromBattleDeath` when the copied body armour reaches 0. [Auto playthrough](Done/auto-playthrough.md) already walks into that detection and wakes in the cave. This story leaves that fight as it is.
- Small stands 100 cm, at `FSolidRaptor::SmallDrawnBodyFactor` of 6. Each raptor holds Claws and Hide. Power is `SolidMonster::PowerFor` of Small. Speed starts at 1. Hide starts at Strength 20. Rags start at Strength 70. The opening loadout is the dented helmet, Rags on the body, and the crude spear in the main hand.
- `SolidInventory::Clear` is the wake's inventory: Rags on the body, and the helmet, the spear, and anything else they were carrying gone from the captain. The stronghold chest stays. The gear left at the death site is [Gear left at the death](gear-left-at-the-death.md). This story does not place that pile.
- A battle death already records Killed by monsters through `NotifyCaptainKilled`. That event enables the cave, clears the inventory, unlocks Meagre, disables Starter, and enables Attack and Defend. `SyncBattlePlans` then assigns the enabled plans in catalog order, so the selection is Attack, then Defend, and the active plan is Attack. `EnableCave` sets `bEnterStronghold`, and `PresentEventResult` enters the stronghold. `WakeFromBattleDeath` returns on a later call because `bWokeFromBattleDeath` is set, so that later death keeps the gear and stays where it is. This story runs the gear loss and the wake from the death itself, every time, including a death outside a fight. The once-only event is not that landing, and the flag does not skip it. FirstDeath does not take it. Meagre stays on the death, as [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) has it. The bridge is the first time, and the player chooses when to walk into it.
- The cave mouth ignores a click while `bStrongholdsEnabled` is false. `EnableCave` and `EnableStrongholdSize(Meagre)` both set that flag on Killed by monsters. [Return to the stronghold](return-to-stronghold.md) is still Ready. It replaces `EnableCave` with `ReturnToStronghold` on that event. This story puts the trip on every captain death, so a later death still enters the stronghold the company has then. The first one is the cave. The squad cap is still 0, so this first death has no companion out. Entering the stronghold is a closed visit, and a closed visit despawns companions who were out. The squad's selection is kept. The leave uses the Build squad check from [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md).
- Driven from the village still opens the Iglin tunnels. Today its `Require` names `IdKilledByMonsters`. This story names `IdFirstDeath`, so the tunnels stay shut while the raptors are left alone. Enter Relion still repairs the bridge. With both the bridge broken and the tunnels shut, the river is the edge of the world the captain can reach. Rethil is not in this story. [Basic NPCs](basic-npcs.md) does not name him either.
- Reach broken bridge is `IdReachBrokenBridge` (`a17c4e58`), trigger `ETrigger::ReachBrokenBridge`. It requires Enter Iglin and carries no change. `NoteBrokenBridgeRamp` calls `NotifyReachBrokenBridge` when the captain stands on an Iglin ramp. The ramp records the event and shows nothing. The monster message remains `SolidMessages::IdKilledByMonsters` (`7c2e9a41`), text "You are killed by monsters". Nothing in play shows it. Killed by monsters (`e51b8c36`) is still the close of that message, and the battle death also marks that event complete. Today the event enables the cave, clears the inventory, unlocks Meagre, disables Starter, and enables Attack and Defend. This story removes Reach broken bridge. [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) says standing on the Iglin ramp still fires it. [Auto playthrough](Done/auto-playthrough.md) may step on the Relion-side ramp, and that step then records nothing.
- FirstDeath is a new definition, `IdFirstDeath`, trigger captain death, name `First death`, ACT_0. `ETrigger` gains that trigger. The captain's death notifies it. The once-only rule is the one every event already has. Its changes are `DisableBattlePlan(Starter)`, `EnableBattlePlan(Attack)`, and `EnableBattlePlan(Defend)`. Those three changes leave Killed by monsters. The journal phrases are the ones `SolidJournal::Describe` already builds: `Starter disabled`, `Attack enabled`, and `Defend enabled`. Driven from the village's `Require` names `IdFirstDeath` instead of `IdKilledByMonsters`. The portal still uses `MarkComplete` on Driven from the village, so it still records that event when FirstDeath has not fired. The plan changes still happen on the death [Auto playthrough](Done/auto-playthrough.md) already waits through, on FirstDeath.
- Killed by monsters keeps the message id. Nothing in this story shows that message. The gear loss and the wake run from the death, every time, so a later death still loses the gear and still wakes in the current stronghold.
- Tests that reach the later chain by standing on the ramp, or by calling `NotifyReachBrokenBridge`, notify FirstDeath from a captain death instead. `SolidCore1.Events.ReachBrokenBridge` goes. Tests that close the monster message to enable Attack and Defend, including `SolidCore1.BattlePlan.*`, notify FirstDeath the same way. Closing the message leaves those plans as they are. A FirstDeath test checks the one fire, the plan changes, and Driven from the village's new requirement. A later captain death still clears the inventory and still enters the stronghold.
