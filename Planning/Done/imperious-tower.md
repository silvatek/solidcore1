# Imperious Tower

A stone tube on the grassland cell 17,31, centered on that cell. Ten courses taper from 10 m across at the grass to a flat 6 m cap at 30 m. Each course starts at the width the course below ends, so the side is smooth. A dark door, 1.4 m by 2.4 m, faces north. The captain can walk up to it. A click does not enter.
