# Iglin Bridge Fight Enhancements

Status: Ready

## Story

The captain starts in Iglin. Three Small raptors wait on the grass beside the broken bridge. The player can leave them alone. This is an open world. When the captain comes into the fight, those three kill the captain. The captain may kill one of them first. That death still comes, and running away does not end the fight.

Any death of the captain, at any point in the game, lands them in their current stronghold without the gear they had when they died. This fight is the first time that happens. Until that death, the company stronghold stays locked, the Iglin tunnels stay locked, and the broken bridge still blocks the river. The current stronghold, once that death opens it, is the cave. A later story adds the NPC Rethil, who guides the player toward the bridge. This story does not.

Reach broken bridge is removed. Standing on the bridge records nothing and shows nothing. The message "You are killed by monsters" is removed.

The first time the captain dies, Killed by monsters fires. Its trigger is FirstDeath. Like every event, it fires once. A later death does not fire it again. It disables Starter and enables Attack and Defend. Driven from the village still requires it.

Every captain death returns them to the company stronghold and clears the inventory. A later death does that too. The once-only event does not.

This story depends on [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md), which is Done. That story ships first. [Combat preparation](Done/combat-preparation.md) is Done, and it ships before Basic combat.

This story introduces no game concept. Raptor, the encounter, the captain's death, and the event list are already named. FirstDeath is a trigger. Killed by monsters is the event that uses it.

## Acceptance

- The Iglin Bridge encounter still places three Small raptors on the Iglin side of the broken Iglin–Relion bridge, as [Basic monsters](Done/basic-monsters.md) placed them.
- The fight starts as [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) says, when the captain comes into DetectionRange. Staying outside that range leaves the raptors idle. From the moment the fight starts, the three Small raptors kill the captain. Killing one of them leaves the fight as those three, and the fight ends on the captain's death.
- [Leave a fight](leave-a-fight.md) is where EscapeDistance ends a fight. This encounter does not end. The three Small raptors keep fighting until the captain dies.
- Any death of the captain, in this fight or later, anywhere in the game, uses one landing. The captain wakes in the company stronghold they have then, without the gear they had when they died. The first time, that stronghold is the cave. The wake is the one [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) specifies: Rags on the body, the rest of the inventory emptied, and the companions who were out despawned. The leave uses that story's Build squad check. The gear on the ground is [Gear left at the death](gear-left-at-the-death.md).
- A later captain death uses that same landing. Killed by monsters has already fired, and the death still returns them to the stronghold the company has then and still clears the inventory to Rags.
- Until that death, the cave mouth stays shut, the Iglin tunnels stay shut, and the Iglin–Relion bridge stays broken. The river still cannot be crossed there, so the wider world stays out of reach. The death opens the stronghold. The tunnels still wait on Driven from the village. The bridge still waits on Enter Relion.
- This story does not add Rethil. A later story adds that NPC, who guides the player toward the bridge.
- Reach broken bridge is removed. Standing on either ramp of the Iglin bridge records nothing and shows nothing.
- The message "You are killed by monsters" is removed. `SolidMessages` has no row for it. Closing a message does not fire Killed by monsters.
- Killed by monsters keeps the id `e51b8c36`, the name `Killed by monsters`, and ACT_0. Its trigger is FirstDeath. The captain's first death fires it. The journal names it `Killed by monsters`. A second death leaves it unfired and leaves its changes as they are.
- Killed by monsters disables Starter and enables Attack and Defend. The squad's selection becomes Attack, then Defend. The active plan becomes Attack. The battle-plan list shows F1 Attack and F2 Defend. The journal records `Starter disabled`, `Attack enabled`, and `Defend enabled`. It still enables Meagre. It does not clear the inventory, and it does not travel.
- Driven from the village still requires Killed by monsters. The portal to the grass north of the Iglin Tunnels Entrance still records Driven from the village when that event has not fired.
- Enter Relion still repairs the bridge. The center is drawn. The road stays `Iglin -> Relion, track`.

## Implementation notes

- The catalog row stays three Small raptors. It is the `FStaticEncounter` named Iglin Bridge in `SolidEncounter::StaticEncounters`: one group, size Small, count 3, plan Attack, and no event id. The fight adds no member. [Place encounter markers](Done/place-encounter-markers.md) can still move the focus onto a chart mark. The band, the separation, and the count of three stay.
- `SolidCore1.Battle.IglinBridge` already runs this fight. The squad plan is Starter, the loadout is `SolidInventory::StartingLoadout`, and the group is that row. The second spear kills one Small. The next claw kills the captain. The other two abort. The group's hide stays above 0. `AdvanceBattle` calls `WakeFromBattleDeath` when the copied body armour reaches 0. [Auto playthrough](Done/auto-playthrough.md) already walks into that detection and wakes in the cave. This story leaves that fight as it is.
- Small stands 100 cm, at `FSolidRaptor::SmallDrawnBodyFactor` of 6. Each raptor holds Claws and Hide. Power is `SolidMonster::PowerFor` of Small. Speed starts at 1. Hide starts at Strength 20. Rags start at Strength 70. The opening loadout is the dented helmet, Rags on the body, and the crude spear in the main hand.
- `SolidInventory::Clear` is the wake's inventory: Rags on the body, and the helmet, the spear, and anything else they were carrying gone from the captain. The stronghold chest stays. The gear left at the death site is [Gear left at the death](gear-left-at-the-death.md). This story does not place that pile.
- A battle death already records Killed by monsters through `NotifyCaptainKilled`, which `MarkComplete`s `IdKilledByMonsters`. That event enables the cave, clears the inventory, unlocks Meagre, disables Starter, and enables Attack and Defend. `SyncBattlePlans` then assigns the enabled plans in catalog order, so the selection is Attack, then Defend, and the active plan is Attack. `EnableCave` sets `bEnterStronghold`, and `PresentEventResult` enters the stronghold. `WakeFromBattleDeath` returns on a later call because `bWokeFromBattleDeath` is set, so that later death keeps the gear and stays where it is. This story takes `ClearInventory` and the trip off the event. Every captain death runs them, the first and each later one, in a fight or outside one. `bWokeFromBattleDeath` does not skip them. Meagre, the Starter disable, and the Attack and Defend enables stay on the event, so they run once. The bridge is the first time, and the player chooses when to walk into it.
- The cave mouth ignores a click while `bStrongholdsEnabled` is false. `EnableCave` and `EnableStrongholdSize(Meagre)` both set that flag today. Meagre stays on Killed by monsters. [Return to the stronghold](return-to-stronghold.md) is still Ready. It replaces `EnableCave` with `ReturnToStronghold` on the message close. This story removes that message, so the close is gone. The trip runs from every captain death. When that story has shipped, the death calls `ReturnToStronghold` and the event does not. The first stronghold is the cave. The squad cap is still 0, so this first death has no companion out. Entering the stronghold is a closed visit, and a closed visit despawns companions who were out. The squad's selection is kept. The leave uses the Build squad check from [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md).
- Driven from the village still opens the Iglin tunnels, and its `Require` still names `IdKilledByMonsters`. The event now fires from the first death, so the tunnels stay shut while the raptors are left alone. Enter Relion still repairs the bridge. With both the bridge broken and the tunnels shut, the river is the edge of the world the captain can reach. Rethil is not in this story. [Basic NPCs](basic-npcs.md) does not name him either.
- Reach broken bridge is `IdReachBrokenBridge` (`a17c4e58`), trigger `ETrigger::ReachBrokenBridge`. It requires Enter Iglin and carries no change. `NoteBrokenBridgeRamp` calls `NotifyReachBrokenBridge` when the captain stands on an Iglin ramp. The ramp records the event and shows nothing. This story removes that event. [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) says standing on the Iglin ramp still fires it. [Auto playthrough](Done/auto-playthrough.md) may step on the Relion-side ramp, and that step then records nothing.
- The monster message is `SolidMessages::IdKilledByMonsters` (`7c2e9a41`), text "You are killed by monsters". The event `SolidEvents::IdKilledByMonsters` (`e51b8c36`) is a separate id. Today that event is `OnMessageClosed` of the message, and `NotifyCaptainKilled` also marks it complete. This story deletes the message: the id, the text, and every show or close of it. A search of `Source` does not find `7c2e9a41` or "You are killed by monsters". `ETrigger` gains `FirstDeath`. The event is built with that trigger, keeps `e51b8c36`, the name `Killed by monsters`, and ACT_0, and carries no `MessageId`. Its changes are `EnableStrongholdSize(Meagre)`, `DisableBattlePlan(Starter)`, `EnableBattlePlan(Attack)`, and `EnableBattlePlan(Defend)`. `ClearInventory` and `EnableCave` leave it. The captain's death notifies `FirstDeath` the way `NotifyReachBrokenBridge` notifies its trigger. The first death fires the event. A later death notifies it and the event stays unfired. The journal phrases for the plan changes are the ones `SolidJournal::Describe` already builds. The portal still uses `MarkComplete` on Driven from the village. [Auto playthrough](Done/auto-playthrough.md) still wakes in the cave with Rags and empty hands, and it still does not show or close a monster message. The plan changes still happen on that first death.
- Every captain death clears the inventory with `SolidInventory::Clear` and enters the company stronghold. The first death does both, and then the event fires. A later death does both again, and the event stays as it was.
- Tests that reach the later chain by standing on the ramp, or by calling `NotifyReachBrokenBridge`, notify `FirstDeath` from a captain death instead. `SolidCore1.Events.ReachBrokenBridge` goes. Tests that close the monster message, including `SolidCore1.BattlePlan.*` and `SolidCore1.Events.Message`, notify `FirstDeath` the same way. The message id and the text "You are killed by monsters" leave those tests. A FirstDeath test checks the one fire, the plan changes, Meagre, and that a second death leaves the event unfired while still clearing the inventory and still entering the stronghold. The product commit updates the `SolidCore1.Events.*` and `SolidCore1.BattlePlan.*` lines in `Source/SolidCore1/Tests/README.md`. The wake on the first battle death is already `SolidCore1.Battle.DeathWakesTheCaptain`.
