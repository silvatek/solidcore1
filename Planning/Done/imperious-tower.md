# Imperious Tower

A stone tube on the grassland cell 17,31, centered on that cell. Ten courses taper from 10 m across at the grass to a flat 6 m cap at 30 m. Each course starts at the width the course below ends, so the side is smooth. A dark door, 1.4 m by 2.4 m, faces north. A click on that door enters. Inside, each floor is a stone disc with the arrival hole, the stair treads, and the yellow hex grid. The curved wall follows the taper, and the top is closed. The captain arrives alone on the hex nearest the door, looking in. A click on the door inside leaves him on the grass just outside this door. Companions wait outside. F10 Enter cave still opens the Meagre den, and does nothing while he is in the tower. Furnishings wait.
