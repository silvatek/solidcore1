# Auto playthrough

Status: In Progress

## Story

The player opens the F10 menu and chooses Auto playthrough. That starts AutoPlaythrough, which plays the opening of the game from a script. The player watches. The script is a list of steps, and a later story can change the list. This story ships the first list.

The row can be chosen at the very start of a new game, while the captain is still in Iglin and the playthrough has not been started. Choosing it starts the script and dims the row for the rest of that game. Moving away from Iglin dims it too, when it was not already dim. A playthrough that has already started keeps running when the script walks him out.

AutoPlaythrough is the game concept this story introduces. It is the class `FSolidAutoPlaythrough`.

The first script starts from a new game, with the captain in Iglin.

He walks from Iglin up to the monster encounter beside the Iglin bridge, and he stays off the ramp. The battle kills him, and he wakes in the cave. He builds a torch from the chest and puts it in his main hand. He leaves the cave and walks back to Iglin. Iglin casts him out. He closes "You are driven from the village" and walks to the Iglin tunnels. He passes through them and leaves by the far mouth. He walks to Relion, where Sam joins and he is in raven sight. He walks to Kanfold, where Alex joins. He walks to Dragongate, where the company gains Max, Morgan, and Quinn and the squad cap becomes the whole company. He walks to the Imperious stronghold site and constructs an Imperious Tower. He climbs to the top floor, returns to the ground floor, and leaves. Build squad is open, and he selects Sam, Alex, Max, Morgan, and Quinn.

## Stages

Agree this order before a stage is planned in detail. A stage is played before the next stage is planned. The story stays In Progress until the whole script has been played and agreed.

1. **The runner and the death.** The menu row, including the dim row once the playthrough has started or the captain has left Iglin, AutoPlaythrough, and a script that can walk and stop. The steps are the walk from Iglin to the monster encounter, and the wake in the cave.
2. **The torch and the village.** Craft the torch, put it in the main hand, leave the cave, walk to Iglin, and close the driven-out message. Build squad stays closed on that leave.
3. **The tunnels and the company.** Through the tunnels, then Relion, Kanfold, and Dragongate. This stage waits until [Events add a companion](Done/events-add-a-companion.md) is Done. Relion already adds Sam. Kanfold's Alex and Dragongate's Max, Morgan, and Quinn are that story.
4. **The tower and the squad.** The Imperious site, the climb to the top floor, the leave, and selecting the squad.

## Acceptance

Choosing Auto playthrough on a new game runs the script to the end.

- The F10 rows, from the top, are Use portal, Journal, Build squad, Character editor, Auto playthrough, Credits, and Exit Game. Credits stays the row before Exit Game. The new row reads **Auto playthrough**.
- At the start of a new game, while the captain is still in Iglin and the playthrough has not been started, the row can be chosen. Choosing it closes the menu, starts the script, and dims the row.
- The row stays dim for the rest of that game after the playthrough has been started. Stopping the script, including with Esc while he is still in Iglin, leaves the row dim. Confirming the dim row does nothing, and the menu stays open.
- Once the captain moves away from Iglin without having started the playthrough, the row stays dim for the rest of that game. Walking back into Iglin leaves the row dim.
- A playthrough that has already started keeps running when the script walks him out of Iglin. Leaving Iglin is a step, and it is not a stop.
- If he makes no progress toward the current point for 4 seconds, the playthrough stops where he is. The HUD line goes, and the menu stays closed.
- The HUD shows `Auto playthrough` while the script runs. The line goes when the script finishes, and when the player stops it.
- The player's Esc stops the playthrough. The captain stays where he is, and the menu stays closed. The player's F10 stops it and does not open the menu. After the playthrough has stopped, F10 opens the menu as before.
- While the script runs, the player's move, look, and clicks leave the captain on the script. The script walks in true sight, and clicks the ground in raven sight.
- The script walks. It leaves the portal list alone.
- From Iglin he walks up to the Iglin Bridge encounter and stays off both ramps. A raptor detects him, the battle starts, and he waits. The battle kills him. He is inside the cave, with Rags on his body and empty hands. The monster message does not show.
- In the cave he takes one stick and one pitch from the chest, crafts one torch, and puts that torch in the main hand. The rusty sword, the old boots, and the coins stay in the chest. Rags stay on the body.
- He leaves by the dark opening. Build squad stays closed. There are no companions, and the squad cap is still 0. He walks to Iglin.
- Entering Iglin shows "You are driven from the village". He closes it. The Iglin tunnels are open. He enters at the X mouth, follows the straight run, and leaves at the Y mouth.
- He walks to Relion. Sam is in the company and in the squad. The captain is in raven sight, and he stays in raven sight.
- He walks to Kanfold. Alex is in the company and in the squad, beside Sam.
- He walks from Kanfold to Dragongate by way of Relion. Enter Relion stays as it was. At Dragongate, Max, Morgan, and Quinn are in the company. The squad is still Sam and Alex. The squad cap is the company.
- He walks to the stronghold site at row 16, column 36, clicks the stone, and chooses Imperious Tower. He arrives inside, on the ground floor.
- He climbs the stair to floor 10, walks back to the door on floor 1, and leaves onto the grass outside that door.
- Build squad is open on that grass. He places Max, then Morgan, then Quinn into the empty squad boxes. Sam and Alex stay in their boxes. He closes the page. The squad is Sam, Alex, Max, Morgan, and Quinn. The playthrough has stopped, and the HUD line is gone.

## Implementation notes

- `FSolidAutoPlaythrough` is a plain C++ class in `Playthrough/SolidAutoPlaythrough.h`. The script is the static list `Script` at the top of the cpp. Each row is an `FStep` built by a header helper: WalkTo, CloseMessage, CraftItem, ExitStronghold, WakeInStronghold, or Custom. That helper fills the type and the fields that type reads. `Advance` walks the list. A later story edits the list and leaves the class. A death waits on `WakeInStronghold` until `HasPlacedCaptainInStronghold` is set. A custom row carries a constant, and the custom switch matches that constant.
- The game instance holds the object. `OpenLevel` destroys the game mode on the cave trip, the tunnel trip, and the tower trip. The company and the play state survive on the instance. The new mode steps the same object. A script kept only on the mode would vanish when he wakes in the cave.
- One flag on the play state dims the row. Choosing the row sets it, and so does `OccupiedTown` leaving Iglin, including a portal. Spawn sets `OccupiedTown` from none to Iglin and does not set the flag. The flag stays set. The running script does not treat the departure as a stop. The row uses the same dim drawing as Build squad, and confirm does nothing.
- The row goes before Credits, as [Reorder the game menu](Done/reorder-game-menu.md) requires. Key 5 confirms Auto playthrough, key 6 confirms Credits, and key 7 confirms Exit Game. Key 8 has no row. `SolidCore1.MainMenu.Order` already allows a row before Credits and already checks key 8. `SolidCore1.Exit` already requires Credits immediately before Exit Game. Those tests stay. The product commit adds the new label and its action.
- The player's Esc and F10 are the stop. Stage 2 calls `CloseMessage` and the inventory close. Stage 1 does not. The squad page stays closed on the cave leave, because there are no companions. Later stages call `CloseMessage`, the inventory close, and the squad-page close. The script does not send Esc or F10. After a start, the dim row does not start the script again.
- He walks at his normal speed, 500 cm/s. True sight holds the right mouse button and drags the view level along the walk, then holds W through that key's mapping. The drag is a small step each frame, 180 degrees a second, converted to mouse pixels so the 2.5 look scale and the inverted pitch apply that step once. A hitch cannot turn more than a twentieth of a second of that rate. Raven sight moves the pointer onto the ground point and left-clicks. There is no navigation mesh. Follow a road one cell at a time, from `SolidRoad`. A straight line across the map stops on a tree or a building. If the distance to the current point does not fall for 4 seconds, stop where he is and clear the HUD line. A cell is 8 m, so 4 seconds is more than twice a clear step.
- Iglin to the encounter is town and grass. Stop inside a raptor's detection, off the ramps. The broken deck stays closed, and this script does not cross it. The cave trail, the grass in front of each tunnel mouth, Relion, the Kanfold highway, the Dragongate road, and the grass south of Dragongate to the site are the other walks.
- The death is the Iglin Bridge fight. The walk ends when a placed member detects the captain. Steering stops, so the battle can run. `WakeFromBattleDeath` fires Killed by monsters without showing the message, and `PresentEventResult` enters the stronghold. Standing on a ramp still shows that message, so the walk never steps onto one. [Iglin Bridge Fight Enhancements](iglin-bridge-fight-enhancements.md) still replaces the message. This script already uses the fight.
- The Kanfold walk back to Dragongate follows the highway to Relion and the road on to Dragongate. Stepping onto Relion again leaves Enter Relion fired once.
- The site line is `! 16, 36 = StrongholdSite (Imperious)`. The choice row is `Imperious Tower`. Tower is the built type at that size. Call `OfferStrongholdSite` and `ConfirmStrongholdChoice` once he is at the stone.
- The tower is one interior. Floor 1 is the ground and floor 10 is the top. The floor pitch is 300 cm and the riser count is 17, so a riser is about 17.6 cm. The captain's step height stays at the engine default, 45 cm, the flight is 140 cm wide, and the capsule radius is 42 cm. The script walks points along that flight from the tower plan, up to floor 10 and back to the door. One destination on the top floor does not climb the spiral. The script does not travel from floor to floor.
- Inside the cave and the tunnels the open-world footing check is off. The cave walk uses the Meagre floor. The tunnels follow the straight run from the X mouth to the Y mouth.
- Killed by monsters enables Attack and Defend and disables Starter. The squad cap stays 0. There are no companions. Attack and Defend are already in the default catalog, so leaving the cave leaves Build squad closed. Stage 2 does not open that page.
- The torch recipe is one stick and one pitch. The chest starts with five sticks and three pitch. The script opens the inventory, calls `SolidInventory::Move` and `Craft`, puts the torch in the main hand, and closes the page. A torch in the hand lights the tunnels. On the last squad page it calls `SolidSquadBuilder::Drop` for Max, then Morgan, then Quinn, and closes the page. The pages are on screen. The script does not drag with the pointer.
- A test that needs no world reads the step names in order, the menu row, the flag that dims that row after a start and after leaving Iglin, and the mouse pixels a right-button drag uses for a known yaw and pitch. The walk is what PIE shows.
- Do not use the word den.

### Stage 1 — the runner and the death

Played before stage 2 is planned. This stage is the menu row, the dim flag, and a script that walks to the monster encounter and stops when the battle has put him in the cave.

- `FSolidAutoPlaythrough` is the class in `Playthrough/SolidAutoPlaythrough.h`. The game instance holds one by value. OpenLevel keeps that instance, so the cave trip keeps the same object. The captain's tick steps it on the live game mode.
- The first two rows of `Script` are Walk to the monster encounter (`WalkTo`) and Wake in the cave (`WakeInStronghold`). The words are the row's `Name`. A later stage edits `Script` and leaves the class.
- `USolidGameState::bAutoPlaythroughDim` starts clear. `Start` sets it and begins at the first step. `NotifyOccupiedTown` sets it when the occupied town leaves Iglin for any other id, including none and a portal. The spawn arrives from none and leaves the flag clear. The flag stays set. The running script keeps going when that departure is recorded.
- The row label is `Auto playthrough`, the item is `AutoPlaythrough`, and the action is `StartAutoPlaythrough`. It sits after Character editor and before Credits. Key 5 confirms it, key 6 confirms Credits, and key 7 confirms Exit Game. Key 8 has no row. The row uses the same dim drawing as Build squad. `CanStart` is false while the flag is set. Confirming a dim row returns before the menu closes.
- Choosing a live row closes the menu and calls `Start`. The HUD draws `Auto playthrough` at the bottom of the screen while `IsRunning`, with the debug panel shown or hidden. `Stop` clears that line and leaves the menu closed. The flag stays set.
- The player's Esc and F10 call `Stop`. They leave the menu closed and leave a message on screen. After the script has stopped, F10 opens the menu as before. While the script runs, move, look, mouse look, sprint, jump, and clicks leave the captain on the script. This stage does not close a message. The script does not send Esc or F10.
- He walks at 500 cm/s. True sight calls `DragRightMouse` toward the yaw of the walk. That holds the right button and turns through the same mouse-look path as a drag, leveling the view as it goes, then `SimulateKey` for W. That key runs the mapping modifiers and `Move`, the WASD handler. The step is capped, and the pixels undo the 2.5 look scale and the inverted pitch, so one frame cannot spin the view. The button comes up when the view has arrived, when steering stops, and when the script stops. Raven sight calls `SimulateLeftClick` on the ground point. The player's own keys and clicks stay ignored. A new game is true sight, so this stage walks that way.
- The walk follows the Iglin–Relion road one cell at a time, along the painted strip, from the cell nearest the captain toward the bridge. Road points stop before either Iglin ramp. The last point is inside the detection of the Iglin Bridge member closest to Iglin, on the Iglin side of that member, and off the ramps. A straight line that would cross a ramp is slid sideways until it stays on the grass. A step that would land on a ramp is not taken.
- A placed member of that encounter detecting the captain ends the walk. Steering stops. The battle then runs on the captain's tick. The wake step calls `Stop` once the captain has been placed in the stronghold interior. Stage 2 advances there instead. The monster message is not shown and is not closed.
- While a point is current, a distance that does not fall for 4 seconds calls `Stop`. Waiting for the encounter to be placed has no current point, and neither does the wake, so those waits do not start the clock.
- A test with no world reads the two step names, the menu row before Credits, the dim flag after `Start` and after leaving Iglin, including a running script that leaving Iglin does not stop, the W A S D sticks after the mapping modifiers, and the mouse pixels for a drag. `SolidCore1.AutoPlaythrough.Keys` no longer aims the actor, because that aim was a direct turn. The walk is what PIE shows.
- This stage leaves the torch, the village, the tunnels, the company, and the tower for later stages.

### Stage 2 — the torch and the village

Stage 1 has been played. This stage crafts the torch, leaves the cave, walks back to Iglin, and closes the driven-out message. Build squad stays closed on that leave. There are no companions. The tunnels, the company, and the tower stay for later stages.

- `Script` keeps the two stage 1 rows, then Walk to the stand (`WalkTo`, the Meagre hexes), Craft the torch (`CraftItem`, page stays open), Put the torch in the main hand (`CraftItem`, equips and closes), Walk to the entrance (`WalkTo`), Leave the cave (`ExitStronghold`), Walk to Iglin (`WalkTo`), and Close the driven-out message (`CloseMessage`). A later stage edits this list and leaves the class.
- Wake in the cave is a `WakeInStronghold` row. It advances once `HasPlacedCaptainInStronghold` is set. It no longer calls `Stop`. The monster message stays unshown. Placement, the inventory page, and the village message have no current point, so those waits do not start the stall clock. Later deaths add another `WakeInStronghold` row.
- The cave walk uses the Meagre floor. Points are hex centers transformed by the interior actor, the same transform as `GetCaptainSpawnWorld`. He walks at 500 cm/s in true sight, with the same right-button drag and W as the road walk, and the same 200 cm arrival. The open-world footing check is already off inside the cave. A furnishing hex is not a point. The fireplace is (2, 0), the bed is (0, -1), and the chest is (2, 1). The stand beside the chest is (1, 1), along (-1, 0), (0, 0), and (1, 0). The flat-to-flat width is 120 cm and the capsule radius is 42 cm, so a neighbor center clears a one-hex piece.
- Craft the torch opens the inventory with `ToggleInventory` once the stand is reached and the page can draw. The chest is on that page because `CompanyChestOpen` is true in the stronghold. The next tick calls `SolidInventory::Move` and `SolidInventory::Craft` on the play state's loadout and `CaveChest`. `Move` carries a whole stack, and `Craft` spends one of each input, so the tick moves the stick cell `Index(1, 1)` into craft slot 0 and the pitch cell `Index(0, 2)` into craft slot 1, crafts once, then moves the remaining stacks back into those chest cells. The sword, the boots, and the coins are never a source. The body slot is never a destination, so Rags stay. The page stays open.
- Put the torch in the main hand runs on the next tick, while that page is still open. `Craft` has put one torch in pack cell (0, 0). `Move` takes that cell to `ESlot::MainHand`. `ToggleInventory` closes the page. The pack and the craft slots are empty. The chest holds the sword, the boots, the coins, four sticks, and two pitch.
- Leave the cave walks (1, 1), (0, 1), (-1, 1), (-1, 0), and the entrance cell (-2, 0). The mouth is the west flat of that cell, outside the floor, and its mesh blocks. Reaching the entrance cell clears the walk points. The click tick releases the right button, projects the mouth mesh to the screen, and calls `SimulateLeftClick`. A true-sight click still hits a passage, and `ActivatePassage` calls `ExitStronghold`. A click that leaves `WantsStrongholdExit` set advances to Walk to Iglin in that same tick, so the loaded world resumes there. A miss tries again, and that retry has no current point. Build squad stays closed. `OpenAfterStrongholdLeave` is unchanged. A cap of 0 and the default catalog still leave the page closed, and this leave is that case.
- Walk to Iglin follows the chart trail whose ends are the Meagre Dungeon and Iglin, one painted cell at a time, from the cell nearest the captain toward Iglin. The same drag and W apply. The step ends when `OccupiedTown` is Iglin. That entry fires Driven from the village, shows "You are driven from the village", titles him Outcast, and enables the Iglin tunnels. The dim flag is already set, and this arrival does not stop the script.
- Close the driven-out message waits until the HUD is showing that message, calls `CloseMessage`, then advances. The list ends, so `Advance` calls `Stop`. The HUD line goes, the menu stays closed, and the dim flag stays set. The tunnels are open. He does not enter them.
- A test with no world reads the nine row names and their `EStepType` values in that order, and it runs this `Move` and `Craft` sequence on a cleared loadout and `StartingCaveChest`. The chest counts, Rags, and the torch in the main hand are what that test reads. `SolidCore1.AutoPlaythrough.Menu`, `Dim`, and `Keys` stay. `SolidCore1.SquadBuilder.StrongholdLeave` stays closed for a cap of 0 and the default catalog. The walks, the mouth click, and the inventory page are what PIE shows.
- The product commit bumps `SOLID_BUILD_ID` and `SOLID_BUILD_NOTE`. This plan commit does not.
