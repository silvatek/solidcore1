# Controls

Input Actions and the mapping context can be replaced with Content assets on the character later. If unset, C++ creates transient defaults so PIE works immediately.

## General (all modes)

| Action | Keyboard | Xbox | PlayStation |
|--------|----------|------|-------------|
| Move | WASD (true sight) | Left stick (true sight) | Left stick (true sight) |
| Look | Right mouse button (hold) | Right stick | Right stick |
| Pointer | Mouse | — | — |
| Click to move | Left click (raven sight) | — | — |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel (raven sight) | — | — |
| Jump | Space | A | Cross |
| Sprint | Left Shift | Left stick click | Left stick click |
| True sight / raven sight | F9 (after raven sight is enabled) | Right stick click | R3 |
| Open the menu | F10 | Menu | Options |
| Close the menu | Esc or F10 | Menu | Options |
| Inventory | / | View | Create |
| Debug readout | F12 | — | — |
| Close an event message | Click, Enter, Esc, or F10 | Menu | Options |

## Stronghold

| Action | Keyboard | Xbox | PlayStation |
|--------|----------|------|-------------|
| Enter | Left click the dark opening or the tower door | — | — |
| Leave | Left click the opening or the door | — | — |
| Cave chest | Drag | — | — |
| Arrange furniture | `\` or left click the Arrange furniture plate | — | — |
| Leave arrange mode | `\`, Esc, or left click the plate | — | — |
| Wheel while arranging | Does not zoom | — | — |

While arrange mode is on, WASD, the left stick, and a click do not move the captain or use the door. F10 and / do nothing. The wheel does not zoom.
