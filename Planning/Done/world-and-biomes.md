# World + biomes

128×64 `WorldMap.txt` overlay (Sea, Grassland, Town, Mountain, Forest, Desert, River) scaled onto a 513×513 TerrainPoint map (1024 m side, 200 cm spacing). The world stays square. Each cell is 8 m east–west and 16 m north–south. Named towns are `!` cells: Iglin (start), Relion, Kanfold, Visolar, Jethan, Dragongate (a stone wall across its cells, following the long axis of that town, with a 4 m gate and a tower each side; the wall is 4 m high and the towers stand above it), and Rathen (on the north bank of the southern river). A `!` is one cell, named by `Town`, `Stronghold`, `LairEntrance`, or `LairExit` (row, then column, from 1 at the top-left). A `!` with no line is not a location. The captain starts at location 0. Per-biome height shelf (Sea/River 0, Mountain +10 m, else +1 m). Darker mountain grey. A `Stronghold` ! uses the shelf that stronghold names. Meagre Dungeon, the cave, uses the Mountain shelf. Imperious Tower uses Grassland. A `*` is a 4 m channel through that square, or a 4 m pond when it stands alone. It joins an orthogonal river or the sea and stays 4 m wide. The rest of the square takes the biome beside it, so the banks can differ, and those banks take that biome's shelf. Where two biomes meet, the slope uses the texture of the higher side, so a mountain cliff is rock rather than the river below it.
