# Events

A list of one-shot triggers. Enter town fires when the captain steps onto a named town cell; the parameter is that town's index. Each event has an eight-digit id and an act. The act numbers are the constants ACT_0, ACT_1, ACT_2, ACT_3, and ACT_4. Enter Iglin, Reach broken bridge, and Killed by monsters are ACT_0. Driven from the village is ACT_1. Enter Relion is ACT_2, Enter Kanfold is ACT_3, and Enter Dragongate is ACT_4. Messages are a separate list, each with an eight-digit id. ShowMessage names one. MessageClosed fires when that message is closed. An event can require other ids, and it does not fire until those have. Entering town 0 (Iglin) is recorded and titles the captain Orphan. Reach broken bridge requires Enter Iglin. It fires when the captain stands on either ramp of the Iglin bridge, including after that bridge is repaired. It shows the message "You are killed by monsters". The deck and the gap past the lip do not count. Standing on a ramp before Iglin changes nothing and is not recorded. The next time he stands there after Iglin records it and shows the message. A click, Enter, Esc, or F10 closes that message. Killed by monsters then fires, opens the starting cave, sends the captain inside, and clears his inventory, including the pack, leaving Rags on the body. Driven from the village requires that close. It fires the next time the captain enters Iglin, shows "You are driven from the village", titles the captain Outcast, and opens the Iglin tunnel mouths. It does not send him inside. The portal to the grass north of the X mouth records that same event without waiting for the close. A return to Iglin before that close changes nothing extra and is not recorded. Entering town 1 (Relion) requires Enter Iglin. It then enables raven sight, sets max party size to 1 (Sam), titles the captain Vagrant, and repairs the Iglin bridge. A visit to Relion before Iglin changes nothing and is not recorded. The next visit after Iglin applies it. Entering town 2 (Kanfold) enables raven sight, sets max party size to 2 (Sam and Alex), and titles the captain Ranger. Entering town 5 (Dragongate) enables raven sight, sets max party size to 5 (the whole company), and titles the captain Captain. The nameplate starts as Outcast. F5 does not fire Reach broken bridge. F5 does visit Iglin, so after the close it shows the village message too.
