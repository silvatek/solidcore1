# Inventory

F10 menu, **8 Inventory**, or `/`. The page is the same oak-and-bronze board as the journal. The name sits in the top border. On the left, boxes follow a person: head, body, and boots in a column, the hands beside the body, and a narrower belt between the body and the boots. Head, Body, Off hand, Main hand, Belt, and Boots are headings above those boxes, in the same letters as Pack and Chest. On the right, the captain's pack is six boxes, two columns by three rows, under the heading Pack. The items are Rags (armor, Inferior), a dented helmet (helmet, Inferior), a crude spear (weapon, Inferior), and a torch (tool, Common). Each of them stacks to 1. A worn box or a pack cell stores one of those ids, and the page shows its name. Armor can be dragged only onto the body. A helmet can be dragged only onto the head, boots only onto the boots, and a belt only onto the belt. Any item can be dragged into the pack or a chest. A rusty sword is an Inferior weapon. It stacks to 1. Old boots are Inferior and stack to 1. Coins are a material with no grade and stack to 999. A stick is an Inferior material and stacks to 10. Pitch is a Common material and stacks to 10. The cave chest is two columns by three rows. It starts with the rusty sword, the old boots, 20 coins, five sticks, and three pitch. The last cell is empty. A stack above one shows that count after the name. Press a worn box, a pack cell, or a chest cell that holds an item, and release on another. An occupied destination swaps when both items fit. The chest is on the page only inside the cave, so a drag back into it happens there. A new game wears the dented helmet, Rags, and the crude spear in the main hand. The off hand, the boots, the belt, and the pack start empty. Closing the monster message empties every worn box, the pack, and the crafting slots, then puts Rags back on the body. On the bottom right, four crafting slots sit in a row, with a small bronze Craft button beside them. An item can be dragged into a crafting slot, and back out. Each such drop checks the known recipes. The button lights when one of them is satisfied by those slots and the pack has an empty cell. Otherwise it stays dim. Pressing it spends those ingredients, clearing a stack that reaches zero, and puts one crafted item in the first empty pack cell. The button stays lit when the slots can still make that recipe and the pack still has room. `/` again, Esc, or F10 closes the page. Move, look, and battle-plan keys wait while it is open.
