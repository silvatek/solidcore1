# Cave stronghold

The company's first base, a Meagre Dungeon. A stronghold has a type (Dungeon, Fortress, Tower) and a size (Meagre, Humble, Average, Impressive, Grand, Stately, Imperious). A stone mouth (dark opening, rock jambs and lintel) stands against the cliff between the stronghold cell and the grass next to it, facing that grass, with the captain's standing spot on the grass in front of the stone. The cave starts closed. **Enter cave** and a click on the mouth do nothing until the captain closes the message from Reach broken bridge. Closing it opens the cave and sends him inside. After that, **F10, 6 Enter cave** and the mouth both load the interior: an irregular den of fourteen hexes, captain only. The entrance is wider than the captain. The same dungeon code will serve every size; Meagre is the cave, and a Meagre stronghold has a single floor. A yellow hex grid covers that floor, 1.2 m from flat side to flat side, and the same grid will serve every stronghold type. Furnishings come from one shared list. The cave uses three of them, on level 0: a grey fireplace with a lower red hearth (one hex), a beige bed with a white pillow narrowed so it sits inside its two hexes, and a beige chest with a rounded lid (one hex). The chest is a storage container. It holds a rusty sword, old boots, 20 coins, five sticks, and three pitch. Inside the cave, the inventory page shows that chest. A drag moves an item between a worn box, a pack cell, and a chest cell, and an occupied destination swaps. Outside the cave the chest stays off the page and its contents remain. The captain's nameplate stays hidden in the stronghold. Companions are removed with the open world and spawn again beside him at the mouth on **Leave cave**. The third-person playground stays on that map, and its colorway blocks (the green shapes, and the other colors) are hidden for the visit. The squad builder stays closed. A left click on the dark opening enters from the cliff and leaves from inside. While the pointer rests on that opening it reads **Enter cave** or **Leave cave**. **F10, 6** still makes the same trip. The Meagre Dungeon's visit view aims the camera level: into the den on entry, and out over the grass on leave.
