# Controls

Runtime defaults. C++ creates these when the character has no Content Input Actions, so PIE works before a mapping context is assigned. A Content mapping context on the character can replace them later.

General applies in the open world, in a stronghold, and in a lair. An open page uses that page's table. While a page is open, these world keys wait. During Test Drill, move, look, and sprint wait until the square walk ends.

## General (all modes)

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Move | WASD (true sight) | Left stick (true sight) |
| Look | Right mouse button (hold). The pointer hides while it is held. | Right stick |
| Pointer | Mouse | — |
| Click to move | Left click (raven sight) | — |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel (raven sight). Scroll up lengthens the camera arm. | — |
| Jump | Space | A / Cross |
| Sprint | Left Shift | Left stick click |
| True sight / raven sight | F9, once raven sight is enabled | — |
| Main menu | F10 | — |
| Inventory | / | — |
| Debug readout | F12 | — |
| Close an event message | Click, Enter, Esc, or F10 | — |

The inventory key is `/` with Shift up. F12 toggles the tech readout. Battle plans stay on screen either way.

## Open World

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Battle plan | F1 Line, F2 Column, F3 Tight mob, F4 Loose mob, F5 Parade. F6, F7, and F8 stay empty until a plan is assigned. | — |
| Enter a cave, a tower, or the Iglin tunnels | Left click the dark opening or the tower door | — |
| Test Drill | F10, then 2 | — |

F5 Parade fires Enter Iglin when that visit is still ahead, then Enter Relion and Enter Dragongate, once each. The company walks the active plan beside the captain. The same F-keys change the active plan while a page is closed, including inside a stronghold or a lair, and the company uses it when they are with him again.

Engine view modes sit on Ctrl+F1–F5, so the bare F-keys stay with the battle plans.

## Stronghold

The Meagre cave and a tower are both strongholds. The captain goes in alone. Companions wait on the grass outside.

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Enter | Left click the dark opening or the tower door | — |
| Leave | Left click the opening or the door from inside | — |
| Enter or leave the Meagre cave | F10, then 6. The row reads Enter cave on the grass and Leave cave inside. Enter cave stays closed until the cave is open. Leave cave stays available. | — |
| Cave chest | With the inventory open in the cave, drag between the chest and the worn boxes, the pack, or the craft slots | — |

The cave row is the Meagre cave. A tower uses the door click.

## Lair

The shipped lair is the Iglin tunnels. The captain goes in alone. Companions wait on the grass in front of the mouth he used.

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Enter | Left click the dark opening on the grass, once the tunnels are open | — |
| Leave | Left click the opening inside. That click leaves by the mouth that was hit. | — |

F10, then Use portal, then Iglin tunnels stands the party on the grass north of the entrance mouth. That row stays outside.

## Main menu

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Open | F10 | — |
| Move | Up / Down, or point at a row | — |
| Confirm | Enter, 1–8 on the number row or the numpad, or left click | — |
| Close | Esc or F10 | — |

The rows, in order:

1. Use portal
2. Test Drill
3. Journal
4. Credits
5. Build party. The row stays closed until two companions are allowed.
6. Enter cave, or Leave cave when he is already in the Meagre cave
7. Character editor
8. Inventory

## Party builder

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Open | F10, then 5, once two companions are allowed | — |
| Move | Up / Down, or point at a row | — |
| Toggle that companion | Enter, 1–8, or left click | — |
| Close | Esc or F10 | — |

A companion already in the party is removed. A new one is refused once the event cap is full.

## Inventory

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Open | `/`, or F10, then 8 | — |
| Close | `/`, Esc, or F10 | — |
| Move an item | Press a slot that holds an item. Release on a worn box, a pack cell, or a craft slot. An occupied destination swaps when both items fit. | — |

In the Meagre cave the page also shows the chest, and a drag can use those cells. See Stronghold.
