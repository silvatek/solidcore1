# Town + forest

Random non-overlapping town buildings (grey cuboid + red gable prism roof); forest trees scattered on Forest cells. Dragongate is not that pack: a 4 m stone wall runs across its cells, with a 4 m gap and a taller tower on each side of the gap. A row of cells runs east–west and a column runs north–south. Buildings stay on cells marked `o` or a `!` named as a town, clear of the start-town centroid and of each welcome sign.
