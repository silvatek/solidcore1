# Auto playthrough

Status: In Progress

## Story

The player opens the F10 menu and chooses Auto playthrough. That starts AutoPlaythrough, which plays the opening of the game from a script. The player watches. The script is a list of steps, and a later story can change the list. This story ships the first list.

The row can be chosen at the very start of a new game, while the captain is still in Iglin and the playthrough has not been started. Choosing it starts the script and dims the row for the rest of that game. Moving away from Iglin dims it too, when it was not already dim. A playthrough that has already started keeps running when the script walks him out.

AutoPlaythrough is the game concept this story introduces. It is the class `FSolidAutoPlaythrough`.

The first script starts from a new game, with the captain in Iglin.

He walks from Iglin up to the monster encounter beside the Iglin bridge, and he stays off the ramp. The battle kills him, and he wakes in the cave. He disables the Iglin Bridge encounter, builds a torch from the chest, puts the torch in the off-hand and the rusty sword in the main hand. He leaves the cave and walks back to Iglin. Iglin casts him out. He closes "You are driven from the village" and walks to the Iglin tunnels. He passes through them and leaves by the far mouth. He walks to Relion, where Sam joins and he is in raven sight. He walks to Kanfold, where Alex joins. He walks to Dragongate, where the company gains Max, Morgan, and Quinn and the squad cap becomes the whole company. He walks to the Imperious stronghold site and constructs an Imperious Tower. He climbs to the top floor, returns to the ground floor, and leaves. Build squad is open, and he selects Sam, Alex, Max, Morgan, and Quinn.

- The F10 rows, from the top, are Use portal, Journal, Build squad, Character editor, Auto playthrough, Credits, and Exit Game. Credits stays the row before Exit Game. The new row reads **Auto playthrough**.

## Implementation notes

- `FSolidAutoPlaythrough` is a plain C++ class in `Playthrough/SolidAutoPlaythrough.h`. The script is the static list `Script` at the top of the cpp. Each row is an `FStep` built by a header helper: WalkTo, CloseMessage, CraftItem, EquipItem, DisableEncounter, ExitStronghold, EnterLair, ExitLair, WakeInStronghold, SelectBattlePlan, AimRaven, ChooseStronghold, BuildSquad, or Custom. That helper fills the type and the fields that type reads. `Advance` walks the list. A later story edits the list and leaves the class. A death waits on `WakeInStronghold` until `HasPlacedCaptainInStronghold` is set. A `DisableEncounter` row records that name on the play state so `ChooseMonsters` skips it after the cave trip. A `CloseMessage` row waits one second after the matching message is showing, then closes it. A custom row carries a constant, and the custom switch matches that constant.
- The game instance holds the object. `OpenLevel` destroys the game mode on the cave trip, the tunnel trip, and the tower trip. The company and the play state survive on the instance. The new mode steps the same object. A script kept only on the mode would vanish when he wakes in the cave.
- One flag on the play state dims the row. Choosing the row sets it, and so does `OccupiedTown` leaving Iglin, including a portal. Spawn sets `OccupiedTown` from none to Iglin and does not set the flag. The flag stays set. The running script does not treat the departure as a stop. The row uses the same dim drawing as Build squad, and confirm does nothing.
- The row goes before Credits, as [Reorder the game menu](Done/reorder-game-menu.md) requires. Key 5 confirms Auto playthrough, key 6 confirms Credits, and key 7 confirms Exit Game. Key 8 has no row. `SolidCore1.MainMenu.Order` already allows a row before Credits and already checks key 8. `SolidCore1.Exit` already requires Credits immediately before Exit Game. Those tests stay. The product commit adds the new label and its action.
- The player's Esc and F10 are the stop. Stage 2 calls `CloseMessage` and the inventory close. Stage 1 does not. The squad page stays closed on the cave leave, because there are no companions. Later stages call `CloseMessage`, the inventory close, and the squad-page close. The script does not send Esc or F10. After a start, the dim row does not start the script again.
- He walks at his normal speed, 500 cm/s. True sight holds the right mouse button and drags the view level along the walk, then holds W through that key's mapping. The drag is a small step each frame, 180 degrees a second, converted to mouse pixels so the 2.5 look scale and the inverted pitch apply that step once. A hitch cannot turn more than a twentieth of a second of that rate. Raven sight moves the pointer onto the ground point and left-clicks. There is no navigation mesh. Follow a road one cell at a time, from `SolidRoad`. A straight line across the map stops on a tree or a building. If the distance to the current point does not fall for 4 seconds, stop where he is and clear the HUD line. A cell is 8 m, so 4 seconds is more than twice a clear step.
- Iglin to the encounter is town and grass. Stop inside a raptor's detection, off the ramps. The broken deck stays closed, and this script does not cross it. The cave trail, the grass in front of each tunnel mouth, Relion, the Kanfold highway, the Dragongate road, and the grass south of Dragongate to the site are the other walks.
- The death is the Iglin Bridge fight. The walk ends when a placed member detects the captain. Steering stops, so the battle can run. `WakeFromBattleDeath` fires Killed by monsters without showing the message, and `PresentEventResult` enters the stronghold. Standing on a ramp records Reach broken bridge and does not show that message. The walk still stays off the ramps. This script already uses the fight.
- The Kanfold walk back to Dragongate follows the highway to Relion and the road on to Dragongate. Stepping onto Relion again leaves Enter Relion fired once.
- The site line is `! 16, 36 = StrongholdSite (Imperious)`. The choice row is `Imperious Tower`. Tower is the built type at that size. Call `OfferStrongholdSite` and `ConfirmStrongholdChoice` once he is at the stone.
- The tower is one interior. Floor 1 is the ground and floor 10 is the top. The floor pitch is 300 cm and the riser count is 17, so a riser is about 17.6 cm. The captain's step height stays at the engine default, 45 cm, the flight is 140 cm wide, and the capsule radius is 42 cm. The script walks points along that flight from the tower plan, up to floor 10 and back to the door. One destination on the top floor does not climb the spiral. The script does not travel from floor to floor.
- Inside the cave and the tunnels the open-world footing check is off. The cave walk uses the Meagre floor. The tunnels follow the straight run from the Iglin Tunnels Entrance to the Iglin Tunnels Exit. `EnterLair` and `ExitLair` click those mouths. The run is a `WalkTo` of kind `Lair`.
- Killed by monsters enables Attack and Defend and disables Starter. The squad cap stays 0. There are no companions. Attack and Defend are already in the default catalog, so leaving the cave leaves Build squad closed. Stage 2 does not open that page.
- The torch recipe is one stick and one pitch. The chest starts with five sticks and three pitch. The script opens the inventory, calls `SolidInventory::Move` and `Craft`, puts the torch in the off-hand and the rusty sword in the main hand, and closes the page. A torch in either hand lights the tunnels. On the last squad page it calls `SolidSquadBuilder::Drop` for Max, then Morgan, then Quinn, and closes the page. The pages are on screen. The script does not drag with the pointer.
- A test that needs no world reads the step names in order, the menu row, the flag that dims that row after a start and after leaving Iglin, and the mouse pixels a right-button drag uses for a known yaw and pitch. The walk is what PIE shows.
- Do not use the word den.
