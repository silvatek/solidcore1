# Dracorex Encounter

Status: Ready

## Story

The player meets one Immense raptor where the chart marks the Dracorex encounter. It is the same green Velociraptor as the three Small raptors at Iglin Bridge, drawn at the Immense size. It stands and idles. Walking into it stops the captain. Coming into range starts a fight, and that fight is the battle a monster group already fights. Starting gear dies here. One claw drops the captain, and the crude spear does not break the hide. A battle simulation runs that fight with the captain's starting loadout against the one Immense raptor. Later stories give the captain chances to upgrade that gear. A kit that cuts the hide to nothing kills the raptor and walks away. A captain death here uses the landing any captain death already uses. The fight the captain cannot survive is Iglin Bridge.

The developer places that mark in the World chart mode, the mode the World Editor opens. The Place list offers Dracorex with the other encounter names. Until that mark is saved on the chart, the world has no Dracorex.

Later stories add the events this encounter waits on. There may be many. This story adds none, so a saved mark places the raptor when the open world chooses its monsters.

This story introduces no game concept. Raptor is already `FSolidRaptor`. Encounter is already `FSolidEncounter`. The marker is a mark on the chart.

This is a single phase. The catalog row and the Immense body land together. The chart mark is placed in the World chart mode after that.

## Acceptance

- The catalog gains one encounter named Dracorex. It is one group of one Immense raptor. The plan is Attack. The near limit, the far limit, and the separation stay the definition defaults, 8 m, 40 m, and 4 m.
- Iglin Bridge stays three Small raptors on Attack. Its mark still places those raptors. A catalog row another story has already added stays.
- A mark named Dracorex is the focus. The point is the one an encounter mark already uses: grassland, walkable, clear of a mountain quad, inside the band, clear of a tree and of a foraging pile, facing the mark, yaw only. One member needs one point. A missing mark places none. Two marks named Dracorex are two of that encounter.
- This story gives Dracorex no event dependency. A saved mark places the raptor at startup, with the other encounters that are ready then. Later stories add the dependencies, and there may be many. Those stories name the events. The mark stays on the chart, and the Place list still offers the name.
- The shipped chart stays as it is. The World chart mode lists Dracorex with the other encounter names. A placed mark stores that name. This story does not write the mark.
- The Immense raptor uses the same green Velociraptor mesh and the same idle as a Small raptor. The feet sit on the ground. The scale is uniform.
- Small stays 100 cm, at `FSolidRaptor::SmallDrawnBodyFactor` of 6. An Immense raptor stands 8 m, eight times that body. `FSolidRaptor::ImmenseDrawnBodyFactor` is 48. A later story can change that height after it has been seen in play. The sizes between Small and Immense still draw as Small.
- The capsule fits the scaled body. Its radius and half-height keep the Small proportions, multiplied by that same eight. The Small capsule stays 27 cm by a 50 cm half-height. The click, the fog seeding, the empty nameplate, and the camera stay as they are for a Small raptor. Detection range starts at 5 m.
- The weapon is Claws. The armour is Hide. Power is the Immense size roll. Immense Hide Strength is 500. Small Hide Strength stays 20. Speed is the monster weapon's starting figure. A later story can change the 500 after it has been seen in play.
- Coming into DetectionRange starts the battle. The log uses Raptor and Claws. Walking into the body still stops the captain.
- A blow that reduces Hide to 0 kills the raptor and ends the fight with the captain alive. Starting gear does not land that blow. Immense Hide is 500 and the crude spear's Power is about 12, so the hide stands. One claw, about 126, drops Rags at 70. The captain dies on that blow, through the landing any captain death already uses. Dracorex stays that one raptor.
- `SolidCore1.Battle.Dracorex` simulates that fight. The captain wears the starting loadout, which is the basic gear: the dented helmet, Rags, and the crude spear. No companion is out. The squad plan is Starter. The group is the Dracorex encounter's one Immense raptor. The first blows are the crude spear, then the claws. The spear leaves the hide standing. The claws kill the captain. The battle is finished, the squad defence is 0, and the hide is still above 0.
- The fight that kills the captain once it has started stays on Iglin Bridge, as [Iglin Bridge Fight Enhancements](iglin-bridge-fight-enhancements.md) specifies. This story leaves that encounter's fight there.

## Implementation notes

This story is one phase. The catalog row and the Immense drawn body land together. The developer places the mark afterwards in the World chart mode. `tools\editor.bat` opens that mode. [Encounter enhancements](encounter-enhancements.md) may ship before or after this story. [Insect monsters](insect-monsters.md) may too. Neither is a dependency.

- The encounter name is `Dracorex`. `FindByName` ignores case, so `dracorex` is that same row. Append it. Iglin Bridge stays the first row.
- Encounter enhancements has not shipped. The row is an `FDefinition` in `SolidEncounter::Definitions`. The group is `EMonsterKind::Raptor`, `ESolidMonsterSize::Immense`, count 1, plan `Attack`. The limits stay the defaults on `FDefinition`. The row carries no event id.
- Encounter enhancements has shipped. The row is an `FStaticEncounter` appended to `SolidEncounter::StaticEncounters`. It does not call `Require`. That story stores one event id, and a second call keeps the later id, so this story leaves the id empty rather than spending the slot. Startup places Dracorex with Iglin Bridge. The Place list still reads the catalog, under whichever type name that story left.
- Later stories add Dracorex's event dependencies. There may be many, and those stories name them. An event already stores up to four ids (`MaxRequiresPerEvent`) and waits until every one has fired. Encounter enhancements stores one id. The story that first gives Dracorex more than one dependency is the story that widens the encounter's list. This story leaves the list empty.
- Insect monsters may already have appended Giant ants. Leave that row. Dracorex follows whatever rows are already there. Iglin Bridge stays first.
- `MakeMonster` already sets `Size` from the group and arms Claws and Hide. `PowerFor` of Immense is the power. `FSolidRaptor::Arm` sets Hide Strength from the size. Immense uses `ImmenseHideStrength`, 500. Small and the other sizes keep `HideStrength`, 20. The member's kind stays Raptor. The actor stays `ASolidRaptor`.
- The death the captain cannot avoid belongs to Iglin Bridge. The three Small raptors there kill the captain. Dracorex adds no further raptor. Its battle is the one [Basic combat](Done/basic-combat.md) already runs. The side that reaches Strength 0 loses.
- At `FSolidItem::StartingDraw`, Immense power is the median 12 raised five times by 1.6, about 126. Rags Strength is 70, so one claw blow drops the starting captain. The crude spear is Inferior, median 12. Immense Hide is 500, so that spear does not bring the hide to 0. Both weapons have Speed 1, and the spear is the first blow, so the claw lands on the next blow and the fight is over. That rapid death is the result this story wants. Power, Speed, and Rags stay as they are. Small Hide stays 20. Later stories add the gear upgrades. This story does not. A later story can change the 500 after the fight has been seen in play.
- The simulation is `FSolidBattleDracorexTest` in `SolidBattleSimulationTests.cpp`, beside `FSolidBattleIglinBridgeTest`. It uses `BuildSquad(TEXT("Starter"))`, `BuildEncounter(TEXT("Dracorex"))`, and `SolidInventory::StartingLoadout`. `GetSquadDefence` reads the body armour, so the dented helmet is worn and the defence is the Rags, 70. The first `AdvanceToNextEvent` logs `Captain prepares.` and `Raptor prepares.`, and both defences stay. The next logs `Captain attacks Raptor with Crude spear.` and `Raptor kills Captain with Claws.` Squad defence is 0. Group defence is `ImmenseHideStrength` minus the spear's Power, and that remainder is above 0. `Finished` is true. A further advance adds no blow. `BuildEncounter` reads the catalog. When Encounter enhancements has renamed `Definitions` to `StaticEncounters`, the helper follows that name. The product commit adds this simulation to the `SolidCore1.Battle.*` line in `Source/SolidCore1/Tests/README.md`.
- `FSolidRaptor::DrawnBodyFactor` returns `SmallDrawnBodyFactor` for Small and `ImmenseDrawnBodyFactor` for Immense. The other sizes stay on the Small factor, which is what the default branch does today. `ImmenseDrawnBodyFactor` is 48, eight times 6, so the drawn body is 8 m. That height is the one this story ships. A later story can change the constant after the body has been seen in play. `SmallDrawnBodyFactor` stays 6.
- `ASolidRaptor` builds the capsule at the Small size. `SetRaptor` resizes it when the raptor is Immense: radius and half-height times `ImmenseDrawnBodyFactor / SmallDrawnBodyFactor`, and the capsule centre stays at the half-height so the bottom is the actor origin. A Small raptor keeps 27 cm and a 50 cm half-height. The default actor, before `SetRaptor`, keeps those Small figures. The body mesh scales through `BodyScale` as it does now.
- The World chart mode already fills the Encounter combo from the catalog in `FSolidWorldChartEdMode`. A new row appears there. `PlaceEncounter` already stores a catalog name and refuses a name the catalog lacks. The shipped `/Game/World/WorldChart` asset stays as it is.
- The test that says the catalog has one encounter counts the rows present when this story ships, and expects Dracorex among them: one group, one Immense raptor, Attack, the default limits. Iglin Bridge stays three Small raptors. A test chart with a mark named Dracorex places one Immense raptor at startup. A chart with no Dracorex mark places none of that encounter. `PlaceEncounter` accepts Dracorex and still rejects an unknown name. An Immense raptor's drawn factor is the new constant, its Hide Strength is 500, and a Small raptor's factor stays 6 and its Hide Strength stays 20. The body test still sees the default capsule at 27 cm by a 50 cm half-height, and an Immense `SetRaptor` grows that capsule. The product commit updates the `SolidCore1.Monster.*` line in `Source/SolidCore1/Tests/README.md`. The tests run without a viewport. The same agent writes the row and runs the suite. The developer places the mark in the World chart mode after the row exists.
