
# History

Note: dates are before (BtF) / after (AtF) the fall of Jethan. The orphan was born some time between 140 and 180 AtF.

- __200 BtF__

    The old kingdom is based in a great city (Jethan) in the middle of fertile plains, mountains to the north west, ocean to north east.

- __40 BtF__

    The first wyvern appears in the far south west, and attacks the kingdom. 

- __38 BtF__

    Wyvern defeated.

- __20 BtF__

    First dragon born, the offspring of a basilisk and a wyvern

- __25 BtF__

    Dragons learn to corrupt insects

- __20 BtF__

    Dragons attack with an army of monsters and start pushing into the kingdom. The kingdom ignores the problem until it is too late, and monsters start flooding in. There is a long war, with the kingdom slowly losing ground.

- __5 BtF__

    Guilds are formed to investigate the Dragon’s magical powers.

- __0 AtF__

    Jethan eventually falls and is overrun and ruined. The old kingdom’s surviving people keep falling back to the north, and are eventually pushed up into the mountains. 

- __3 AtF__

    They find a valley with a narrow entrance that they can fortify, and manage to keep the monsters out. The valley becomes the new kingdom, with the main town (Relion) half way up where the valley splits. Smaller towns & villages established even further up the valleys.

- __15 AtF__

    Big battle at the entrance to the valley, boss dragons killed. Most of the old kingdom remains overrun by monsters, but the valley remains safe.

- __50 AtF__

    Dragons lose control of some of the corrupted insects

- __140 AtF__

    A new dragon rises to command of the monsters. He attacks the fort and breaks into the valley, pushing up to the Relion before being defeated.  Due to this close call, the capital is moved to Kanfold right up at the top of the valley. The fort at the bottom is strengthened and relative peace returns.

    The grand dragon has an egg prophesised to have an even more powerful dragon. 

    The master sorcerer manages to turn the core of the egg into solid metal to stop it.

- __170 AtF__

    The new kingdom starts to send troops out of the valley to reclaim the wilderness. 

- __190 AtF__

    New walled towns are established to maintain a foothold in parts of the wilderness, but without the fortifications of the valley kingdom, monsters still roam.

    There are constant skirmishes between troops and monsters.

    Further south the monsters dominate. 

- __195 AtF__

    A new dragon lord, Dracorex, starts to establish its power in the Southern 
    Mountains.

- __200 AtF__

    Monsters destroy the bridge in the spur valley, cutting off the village of Iglin.
