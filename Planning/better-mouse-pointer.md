# Better mouse pointer

## Story

The pointer stays a bronze arrow drawn in the game, tip on the hotspot. The head is the two triangles that meet at the tip. That head is the right shape. A third triangle, the tail, hangs off the notch and down past the head. It does not follow either edge, so it reads as a stub. The tail comes off.

The bronze, the dark rim, the hidden Windows cursor, and hiding the pointer while the right button looks all stay.

Other bronze pointers are drawn beside the head-only arrow so they can be compared before one is kept. The comparison is part of this story. The head-only arrow is the one that ships if none of the others is chosen.

## Prototypes

[`better-mouse-pointer.png`](better-mouse-pointer.png) shows four shapes on the debug-panel grey. The tip is the top of each arrow.

- **Now** — the pointer as drawn. Head plus the tail triangle. Reference only.
- **Head** — the two head triangles. The tail is gone. This is the minimum.
- **Shaft** — the same head, with a short shaft along the arrow in place of the stub.
- **Plain** — one triangle. No notch and no tail.

## Acceptance

- The tail triangle is not drawn.
- The tip stays the hotspot. The Windows cursor stays hidden. The pointer still hides while the right mouse button looks.
- The pointer in the game is Head, or another prototype named in this file.
- The choice is written here before the story moves to Done.
