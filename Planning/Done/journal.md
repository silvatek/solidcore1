# Journal

F10 menu, **3 Journal**. The page is a large board. The name sits in the top border. The columns are headed **Time** and **Summary**, such as `01:24, 200/53 AtF` beside `Enter Iglin`. A line is an Event, a Stronghold, or a Lair. Events are listed in the order they fired, with what each one changed under the entry. Iglin is recorded as soon as the captain is placed there, with Title Orphan under the name. Reach broken bridge is recorded once the captain stands on an Iglin ramp after Iglin, with the message under the name. Killed by monsters is recorded when that message closes, with Cave enabled and Inventory cleared under the name. That trip also records Enter Stronghold. Driven from the village is recorded the next time the captain enters Iglin after that close, and when the portal stands him north of the X mouth, with the message and Title Outcast under the name. Entering or leaving a stronghold, including the cave, is `Enter Stronghold` or `Exit Stronghold`. The line does not name the size or type; an upgrade will be its own entry. Entering or leaving the Iglin tunnels is `Enter Iglin tunnels` or `Exit Iglin tunnels`. The same trip can be recorded again. A new play session advances the date by one day, from **200/45 AtF**. Days run 0–99, then the year increases. Hours and minutes match real time since that session started and do not roll the date. The cave trip keeps the same day and the same clock. Esc or F10 closes the page.
