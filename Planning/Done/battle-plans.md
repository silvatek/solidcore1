# Battle plans

Company catalog and Party assigned slots. F1 Line, F2 Column, F3 Tight mob, F4 Loose mob, F5 Parade (companions in front, facing the captain, far enough to stay in his view). F5 first fires **Enter Iglin** if it has not already, then **Enter Relion** and **Enter Dragongate**, so a fresh launch still has companions, raven sight, and a repaired Iglin bridge. The captain stays where he is. F1–F8 select; HUD battle-plan panel. The square-walk formation drill is the main menu's Test Drill entry and still cycles F1–F4.
